ABOUT WAVY

Music belongs
to everyone.

Wavy is a free music player built around a simple belief: finding the song you need should feel effortless, not interrupted.

Ads break the moment. They cut through a late-night drive, a study session, or the one song that finally says what you mean. Wavy is being made to put listening first—so your music can stay yours.

And some music is better shared. With Wavy Spaces, couples and close friends can listen together from anywhere: start the same song, stay in sync, and make a small room that feels like your own.

Built with care by Sanju, Wavy is for every kind of listener—alone, together, and always free.
